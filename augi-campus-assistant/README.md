# AUGI - Ahmedabad University AI Assistant & Campus Navigator (2026–2027)

AUGI is an intelligent, mobile-first campus companion tailored specifically for **Ahmedabad University** students, faculty, and visitors. Powered by the official **Student Handbook 2026–2027**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **💬 Intelligent AU Assistant (AUGI)**
   - Answers university questions with exact citations from the 2026–27 Student Handbook.
   - Speech-to-Text voice recognition for hands-free queries while walking across campus.
   - Interactive actionable cards inside chat (view location on map, email advisors).

2. **🗺️ Interactive Campus Map & Gate Pathfinder**
   - Faithful 2D layout covering **Central, East, North, and South Campuses**.
   - Direct highlights and directions for all **15 Campus Gates**.
   - Detailed building guides (University Centre, GICT, SAS, AMSOM, Fabrication Shop, Arboretum, Sports Complex).

3. **👥 Major Advisors & Directory**
   - Instant search for CSE, Economics, Management, Physics, Mechanical, and all other AU majors.
   - Direct one-tap email triggers for Programme Chairs, Managers, and Advisors.
   - Official Escalation Matrix for course disputes and registration concerns.

4. **⚡ Student Productivity Toolkit**
   - **Attendance & Grade Drop Simulator**: Live safety calculation for 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0, and 4.0 credit courses with 75% threshold and 60% NP warnings.
   - **AU 4.00 GPA Calculator**: Multi-course SGPA estimator using Ahmedabad University grade point weights.
   - **Academic Calendar Timeline**: Track Monsoon 2026, Winter 2027, Summer 2027 exam dates, Diwali break, and ISP.

5. **🚨 Emergency SOS System**
   - One-tap access to 24-hour Campus Security (`+91.9998800237`), University Doctor (Dr. Gayatri Raval), Anti-Ragging, POSH, and Ambulance (`108`).

---

## 🚀 How to Run

- **Direct in Browser**: Double-click `index.html` or double-click `run_augi.bat`.
- **Deploy Anywhere**: Upload to GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or serve statically with zero external dependencies.
